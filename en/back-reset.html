The moment

Most people get in touch after one of these moments.

  • You stand in front of the mirror in the morning and do not see the person you used to be. You see the one who has become careful.
  • Your child wants to be picked up, and you quickly work out whether your back is up to it today.
  • When playing you are the first to call it off. Not because you do not want to, but because you cannot.
  • You lift a crate of drinks and hear yourself groan like someone thirty years older.
  • You plan your day around your back without even noticing it any more.
  • Someone asks if you are coming along and you say no before you have even thought about it.

That is the point where it stops being about pain. It is about who you want to be in your family. And about not wanting to be the weakest person in the room.

The process

Four steps. Ninety days.

01

Assessment

Ninety minutes, online or in person. Full movement analysis from your feet to your shoulders, plus the force plate data. By the end you know where the chain breaks.

02

Define the goal

We set out what you want to be able to do again in 90 days. Concrete and measurable, not better at some point. Your plan is built backwards from that.

03

Execute and adjust

Three sessions a week, 60 to 75 minutes. Video feedback on your execution, the Sunday check-in, weekly correction. The plan changes with you.

04

Understand

Throughout the whole time I explain what is happening and why. So that by the end you know yourself what your back needs, instead of starting over.

My commitment

Frequently asked

Before you ask.

I have tried everything. Why should this work?

Because everything you have tried so far was passive. Massage, manipulation, injection, rest: something is done to you, the symptom calms down, the cause stays. That is why it came back. Here you first find out why your back reacts the way it does, and then you actively rebuild it. That is a different road, not a better band-aid.

What are the conditions for the 4-week commitment?

Simple: you did the sessions and sent me your feedback and videos. Without your execution I cannot judge what is going on and cannot adjust anything. If you were in and there is still no difference after four weeks, I redo the entire analysis at no cost to you.

Does this replace my physio or my doctor?

No. I do not make diagnoses and do not replace medical treatment. I build you up with training and biomechanics, often continuing exactly where conventional approaches stop. If a medical assessment is needed, I will tell you so openly.

Does this really work online?

Yes, and in one respect even better than in person: on video I see your movement several times, in slow motion and from several angles. Five minutes of watching in a gym does not do that. I explain the setup in the assessment, and you need no skill beyond propping up your phone.

How much time do I have to invest?

Three sessions a week, 60 to 75 minutes each, plus a few minutes of morning routine. No more. If you want to train six times a week, you are in the wrong place.

What equipment do I need?

A gym with dumbbells and a barbell is enough. No special equipment, no machines.

What happens after the 90 days?

You decide. Through the knowledge transfer you know how to keep it up. If you then want to go for performance, we move into Functional Coaching. If not, this was not a subscription but a finish line.
