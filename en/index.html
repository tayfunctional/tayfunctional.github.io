How it works

Four steps. No guesswork.

01

Assessment

We look at where you really stand: movement, history, daily life and goal. In person with force plates, online on a video call.

02

Your plan

That becomes your individual phase plan, with phase starts, deloads and exit criteria. Nothing off the shelf.

03

Execution

You train to plan in the app and give me feedback through comments and video.

04

Adjust and progress

I adjust the next week to your feedback. We progress when you are ready and pull back when life gets in the way.

Running Coaching is leaner: an intro call instead of an assessment, feedback through comments, no video. The full process is on the pages for Functional & Performance Coaching and Rehab Coaching.

Honesty up front

I do not work with everyone.

I put real time into every client. So I would rather filter early than pour energy into someone who does not actually want it. That protects your results and mine.

That is also why I only take on a limited number of new clients. If there is no space right now, I will tell you openly on the intro call.

Read the list honestly. If you recognise yourself in it, save yourself the intro call.

  • 01You are looking for the cheapest coach. There will always be someone cheaper than me.
  • 02You want a plan but will not report back. Without your videos and your feedback I cannot adjust anything.
  • 03You want something done to you instead of understanding what is going on in your body.
  • 04You argue every adjustment instead of trying it first.
  • 05You are looking for the shortcut. There is none, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something.
  • 06You drop out the moment it gets hard. That is exactly where the work starts.
  • 07You want to hear what is comfortable instead of what is true.

If none of this sounds like you, we are probably a good fit.
