4. Fonts (Google Fonts)

For consistent presentation, this website uses fonts from Google Fonts, a service of Google Ireland Limited. When the fonts are loaded, a connection to Google servers is established and the IP address may be transmitted. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Serving the fonts directly from our own server (local hosting) is planned in order to avoid this transfer.

5. Appointment booking (Calendly)

To book an intro call we link to the external service Calendly (Calendly LLC, USA). Only once you click the button and are forwarded to Calendly is your information (for example name, email, preferred appointment) processed there. The booking additionally asks about your enquiry, such as the type of request, timeframe, current limitations or goals, previous attempts and available training time. Diagnoses or medical findings are not requested. No data is transmitted to Calendly from our website itself. Calendly’s privacy terms apply.

6. Videos (YouTube)

On the Back Reset page we embed a video via YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland). We use a two-click solution for this: when the page is loaded, only a locally stored preview image is shown, there is no connection to YouTube and no data is transferred.

Only when you actively click the play button is the video loaded in extended privacy mode (youtube-nocookie.com). This establishes a connection to Google servers and transmits your IP address as well as technical information about your device. A transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out. The legal basis is your consent given by clicking the play button under Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG. Google’s privacy terms apply.

7. External links (Instagram)

Our website contains links to our Instagram profile. When you click them you are forwarded to Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.). We have no influence on the data processing that takes place there.

8. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process your information in order to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR. The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer required.
