What it is about

Technology alone does not solve a problem. Numbers are not a cause. You only find the cause when your story, a trained eye and experience come together.

That is what my assessment is. I combine practice with theory and with more than ten years in sport. I look at your injury history and your biopsychosocial context, meaning everyday life, work, sleep and stress as well. Then I look at how you move and test what fits your problem. I use force plates when they tell us something. Only all of it together gives you a picture you can work with.

I do not just look at the numbers. I look at you.

The process

90 minutes, five steps, one guide.

01

Your story

We start with a conversation: your injury history, what you have already tried and your biopsychosocial context. Meaning what everyday life, work, sleep and your head add to it.

02

Movement analysis

I look at how you move. With my coach’s eye and my experience, not just where it hurts right now.

03

Tests that fit your problem

In person in Ingolstadt the force plates come in when they tell us something about your problem. If not, I use other tests. Online everything runs via video, at the same price.

04

Your guide

Afterwards you get a Google Doc with my input and your success criteria: the points that matter to get out of the pain or the problem.

05

Put it into practice

You work with the guide on your own. Or you start coaching, and I credit the assessment against the first month.

Frequently asked

Before you ask.

How much does the assessment cost?

The assessment takes 90 minutes and costs 350 € one-off, in person in Ingolstadt or online, the price is the same. The intensive package with the assessment and two PT sessions over three days in Ingolstadt costs 600 € one-off.

Is a training program included?

No. You get a guide as a Google Doc with my input and your success criteria, but no program with feedback. Putting it into practice is up to you. If you want someone with you week by week, Functional & Performance Coaching or Rehab Coaching is the right fit.

Who is the assessment for?

For anyone who wants to know what is really going on. Whether it is your back, your Achilles or performance in your sport, recreational or athlete.

Can the assessment be done online?

Yes. Online it runs via video, also 90 minutes, without force plates. The price is the same. Force plates are only available in person in Ingolstadt.

Is the assessment credited if I start coaching afterwards?

Yes. If you start Functional & Performance Coaching or Rehab Coaching afterwards, I credit the 350 € against the first month. With the intensive package, only the assessment is credited, not the PT sessions. Nothing is credited for the 90-day Back Reset or running coaching.

What does the assessment look at?

Your injury history, your biopsychosocial context, meaning everyday life, work, sleep and stress as well, plus a movement analysis and the tests that fit your problem. Your guide comes out of all of it together.

Do I need the force plates?

Not always. Force plates show how much force you produce and how it is split between left and right. I use them in person when they tell us something about your problem. If not, I leave them out and use other tests. What matters is what I take from it with my eye and my experience.

Does the assessment replace a doctor?

No. I do not diagnose and I do not replace medical treatment. If you need to see a doctor first, I will tell you openly.

How do I book the assessment?

Book a free intro call or send me a DM on Instagram. In the call we work out whether in person, online or the intensive package suits you and set a date.
