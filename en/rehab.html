Back to your sport. With a plan built backwards from your goal.

Science and biomechanics instead of guessing and resting. We define your goal and work our way back to it.

What this is about

Resting does not heal you, it makes you weaker. And the next symptom-chasing programme will not get you back into your sport either. What is missing is a plan that knows where it is going.

That is exactly where I start. It always begins with an assessment: using science and biomechanics we look at why your body does what it does. Then we define your goal, the duration and the timeframe, and your plan is reverse engineered from there. Not training into the blue, but thinking backwards from the result.

And because rehab means closer support, I work your feedback in faster than in Functional Coaching. When something changes, your plan changes.

I do not just fix your pain. I show you why it started, and make sure it does not come back.

The process

Five steps, thought backwards from the goal.

01

Assessment

It starts with the full movement analysis, from your feet to your shoulders, not only where it currently hurts. In person, force plates come on top: they show in black and white where strength and control diverge between left and right. Measured data instead of gut feeling.

02

Onboarding, together

We set up the app together and I explain my system: RPE, load management, how you film your videos. Online exactly as in person. You are never left alone.

03

Define the goal

We set out where you want to go, over what duration and in what timeframe. A clear goal is the condition for a plan that holds.

04

Reverse engineering

From your goal I build the path backwards. Every step has a reason and takes you closer to your sport.

05

Knowledge transfer

I share what I know with you. So that after the Rehab Coaching you still know exactly what to do, instead of starting over.

Frequently asked

Before you ask.

Who is Rehab Coaching right for?

For you, if pain or an old injury is slowing you down and you want to get back into your sport. Especially if you feel you have tried everything and still are not getting anywhere.

Does this replace my physio or my doctor?

No. I do not make diagnoses and do not replace medical treatment. I rebuild you with training and biomechanics, often continuing exactly where conventional approaches stop. If a medical assessment is needed, I will tell you so openly.

Is this online or in person?

The coaching runs online so we can work closely together regardless of where you live. Exactly what the scope looks like we match to your goal in the intro call.

Will I be left alone at the start?

No. Before the first session we set up the app together and I explain my system: RPE, load management, filming. For online clients exactly as in person. You only start once you know how everything works.

How fast do I get an answer?

Within 24 hours on weekdays, within 48 hours at weekends. In rehab that matters: when something hurts, you do not want to wait until next week.

What happens once I have reached my rehab goal?

Then you are not finished, you are ready to go. Through the knowledge transfer you know how to keep it up. And if you then want to go all in on performance, we move into Functional Coaching.

Why does rehab cost more than online coaching?

Because more is included. Personal sessions are part of the package, your plan is adjusted every week from your feedback, and your phase plan has phase starts, deloads and exit criteria instead of a fixed program. On top of that you can work in person if you want to. What that is worth for you is something we work out on the intro call.
